## ✨ What it does

| 📡 Your providers | 📺 A real TV guide | 🔧 Fixes your playlist |
|---|---|---|
| Cable, satellite or antenna — found by your zip code. Tick your channels by their logos. Add the free EPGTalk guides and keep just your teams. | Browse hour by hour, click a show for details, or search for it. | Matches the channels in your `.m3u` to the guide. Your playlist stays on your computer. |
| **📲 Straight to your TV box** | **🕓 Always up to date** | **🌙 English · Español** |
| One address to type into TiviMate or your app — it keeps sharing in the background. | A fresh guide every morning, and the app updates itself with one click. | Light or dark look, and large text for easy reading. |
